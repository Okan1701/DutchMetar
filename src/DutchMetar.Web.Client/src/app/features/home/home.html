Welcome to Dutch METAR

Find current aviation weather for airports in the Netherlands. Browse the airport list or search by ICAO code to view the latest METAR observations and TAF forecasts, along with decoded weather details and flying conditions.

Explore historical METAR and TAF reports, and review weather trends such as temperature, dew point, visibility and wind speed. Select an airport from the list to get started.

Weather data is provided by KNMI and may be delayed or incorrect. Do not rely on this site as your primary source for flight planning.
